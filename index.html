How it works

Ordering your school uniform

Find your school

Search our school list to see exactly which badged items your school requires.

Come in and get sized

Pop into Barrhead or Castlemilk and we’ll fit each item properly — far more reliable than a size chart.

We badge and finish

Crests are embroidered in our own workshop, and we’ll shorten sleeves or hems while we’re at it.

Collect — and come back

Grown out of the blazer by January? Bring it in. Letting out and lengthening is what we do.

Busy season

Beat the August rush

July and August are our busiest weeks of the year. If you can, come in before the end of July — you’ll get a quieter fitting, a full run of sizes and a much faster turnaround on badging and alterations.

  • Bring your child — sizes vary a lot between brands
  • Bring the school’s uniform list if you have one
  • Ask about buying a size up with a turned-up hem
