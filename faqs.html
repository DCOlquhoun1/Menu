Frequently asked questions

The questions we get asked most. If yours isn’t here, just phone the shop — we’d rather you asked.

School uniform

Do I need to order in advance?

For most schools, no — come in and buy off the shelf. In July and August we’re at our busiest and popular sizes go quickly, so the earlier in the summer you come, the better the choice and the faster the turnaround on any badging or alterations.

My child’s school isn’t on your list. Can you still help?

Almost always. The list on our school finder shows the crests we hold in stock. If yours isn’t there, bring us the school’s badge artwork (or ask the school office to email it) and we’ll digitise it and badge the uniform to order.

What size should I buy?

Come in and we’ll measure. Brand sizing varies enormously and a chart can’t tell you how a particular blazer sits on a particular child. There’s a rough guide on our schoolwear page if you want a starting point.

Should I buy a size up so it lasts?

It’s a good strategy if the garment is altered rather than left swimming on them. Buy the bigger size, let us turn up the sleeves and hem, and bring it back next year to be let down. It costs less than buying twice.

Can you badge uniform I bought elsewhere?

Usually yes. Bring the garment in so we can check the fabric and the position — some technical and waterproof materials don’t take embroidery well, and we’d rather tell you that before we put a needle through it.

Do you do nursery and leavers hoodies?

Both. Nursery polos, sweatshirts and cardigans from age 2 upwards, and leavers hoodies for P7 and S6 with the class list on the back. For leavers orders, please give us a few weeks in the summer term.

Alterations

How long do alterations take?

Most everyday jobs — hems, zips, taking in — are back within the week. It’s longer in July and August and in the run-up to prom season. Always tell us the date you need it for and we’ll be straight with you about whether it’s achievable.

How much will it cost?

It depends entirely on the garment and the work. We quote when we see it, before we start, and there’s no obligation. Bring it in or call us and describe the job for a rough idea.

Do I need an appointment?

Not for everyday alterations — just come in during opening hours. Bridal, occasionwear and made-to-measure work is by appointment so we can give you the time and the fitting room properly.

How far ahead should I book bridal alterations?

As soon as you have the dress. Bridal work usually needs two or three fittings, and the final one should be close to the wedding. Summer books up early, so please don’t leave it to the last month.

Workwear & personalisation

Is there a minimum order?

No. We’ll embroider one polo shirt if that’s what you need. Larger quantities work out cheaper per item, but you’re never forced into a box of fifty.

Embroidery or printing — which should I choose?

Embroidery for anything that gets heavy wear and washing, and for a premium look: polos, fleeces, jackets, blazers. Vinyl printing for large back prints, squad numbers, fine detail and short runs. There’s a fuller comparison on our embroidery and printing page.

What artwork do you need from me?

A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) is ideal. A high-resolution PNG usually works. Even a clear photo of your signage gives us something to redraw from. Email it to audreyscreations2013@gmail.com.

How long does a workwear order take?

Typically one to two weeks, depending on garment stock and quantity. Reorders are faster because your logo is already digitised and on file.

Visiting the shop

Where are you and is there parking?

Our main shop is at 48 Aurs Drive, Barrhead, Glasgow G78 2LW, with parking nearby. We also have a store at Unit 6, 315 Drakemire Drive, Glasgow G45 9SS.

What are your opening hours?

Barrhead is open Tuesday to Friday 10am–4pm and Saturday 10am–2pm, closed Sunday and Monday. Hours are extended during the summer uniform season, and Castlemilk hours vary — please call ahead if you’re travelling.

How do I pay?

We take card and cash in store. For larger workwear and school orders we’ll usually take a deposit and settle the balance on collection. Ask us about invoicing for school and business accounts.

Can I return or exchange uniform?

Unworn, unwashed items with tags still on can be exchanged — please keep your receipt and come back to us as soon as you can. Personalised and badged items and completed alterations can’t be returned unless there’s a fault, which is exactly why we fit everything before we badge it. This doesn’t affect your statutory rights.
