Getting here

Finding the Barrhead shop

Aurs Drive sits just off Aurs Road in Barrhead, a short drive from Paisley, Newton Mearns and the south side of Glasgow.

By car

Parking is available close to the shop. From Glasgow, follow the A736 into Barrhead and turn onto Aurs Road.

By train

Barrhead station is on the Glasgow Central to Kilmarnock line, a short journey from the town centre.

By bus

Several local services run through Barrhead from Glasgow, Paisley and Newton Mearns.

We’re easier to talk to than to type to

If you’re not sure what you need, phone us and describe it. That’s usually the shortest route to an answer.
