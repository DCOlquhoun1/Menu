Who are we?

Cova started with a young couple who share a passion for hospitality, and a love of great food. With backgrounds in the industry and experience owning our restaurant, we realised an opportunity to bring a fresh approach to event catering. Coming from families already established in the wedding & events sector we understand the importance of delivering an unforgettable dining experience. Our vision is simple, to bring restaurant quality food to your table — exciting, personal and accessible.

What do we offer?

Our At Home Catering brings the Cova experience to your own home — perfect for dinner parties, family gatherings and celebrations of every size. From canapés to full spreads, we tailor every menu to reflect your tastes and preferences.

To ensure the highest quality at an affordable price we take a craft-first approach, making as much as possible in house. From pastries and cakes to sauces, stocks and even butchery, we carefully handle every ingredient from the moment it arrives to the final dish. This commitment to quality allows us to create food that is fresh, flavourful, and truly special – designed to make your occasion unforgettable.
