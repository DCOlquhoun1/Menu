Who are we?

Cova started with a young couple who share a passion for hospitality, and a love of great food. With backgrounds in the industry and experience owning our restaurant, we realised an opportunity to bring a fresh approach to event catering. Coming from families already established in the wedding & events sector we understand the importance of delivering an unforgettable dining experience. Our vision is simple, to reimagine traditional wedding menus with creativity and care, offering restaurant quality food that feels exciting personal and accessible.

What do we offer?

At Cova we believe that great wedding catering goes beyond just serving food – it should be an experience that brings people together. Our menus showcase a variety of examples of what can be achieved for a variety of dining styles, from À la Carte and Family Style Feats to BBQ Hog Roasts, Breakfast Spreads, Lunches or Afternoon Teas. Whatever the occasion, we tailor every menu to reflect your tastes and preferences.

To ensure the highest quality at an affordable price we take a craft-first approach, making as much as possible in house. From pastries and cakes to sauces, stocks and even butchery, we carefully handle every ingredient from the moment it arrives to the final dish. This commitment to quality allows us to create food that is fresh, flavourful, and truly special – designed to make your celebration unforgettable.

Family Style Dining

What is Family Style?

Family style is a warm and interactive way to bring guests together, with generous platters of food served at the table for everyone to share. It creates a relaxed, communal atmosphere, encouraging conversation and connection while allowing guests to enjoy a variety of dishes at their own pace.

For a true family style feast, we suggest 2-3 mains and 3-4 sides, served for guests to share, and enjoy together. From options like grazing platters to start or a shared pavlova for dessert the options for family style are endless. We'll tailor every detail to your taste, creating a menu that feels right for your celebration.

Buffets

What is a Buffet?

A buffet is a self-serve style of catering where guests can help themselves to a variety of dishes, making it a relaxed and sociable way to dine. Our hot buffet offers a flexible and flavourful option ideal for larger gatherings. We've created three themed buffet styles to make your selection simple, but you're also welcome to mix and match dishes to suit your event. To create a balanced and satisfying spread, we recommend choosing 3–4 hot mains, 2–3 hot sides, and 2 cold sides. For our Italian buffet, we suggest selecting 1–2 styles of pizza, 1–2 pasta dishes, and a variety of 3–4 Italian-style bites. Please note: all buffets require a minimum order of 35 people.

Food Stalls

What is a Food Stall?

A food stall is a dynamic and interactive dining option that adds a unique touch to your celebration. These stalls are dedicated stations offering a specific type of food, prepared fresh and served directly to your guests. They create an informal yet elegant dining experience, allowing guests to enjoy personalised dishes while mingling and exploring different flavours.

Examples

Oyster Shucking & Caviar

Up to 3 varieties of local oysters being shucked and served to order, a choice of 'perfect pairings' including homemade hot sauce, pickles and lemons. Washed down with stout, whiskey, or Fino sherry - or classically glasses of bubbly

Cheese & Charcuterie Stall

Homemade and curated cred & cooked meats sliced alongside garnishing pickles & ferments. In addition to a selection of local cheeses, jams, chutneys, honey, fresh baked bread, cherry, port, cheese & fruit. This could be paired with IPA beer tasting / pairing

Pasta / Gnocchi Stall

Featuring freshly prepared gnocchi and a selection of artisan pastas. This interactive station allows guests to customize their plates with an array of rich, flavourful sauces and gourmet toppings. From creamy classics to bold zesty flavours each dish is made to order, ensuring a fresh & personalised experience. This stall combines elegance and warmth, offering a memorable dining experience that your guests will savour long after the last bite.

Pizza Van

Our 1965 VW HY Van brings vintage charm to your wedding, serving artisan pizza by the slice. Freshly baked and perfect for sharing, it adds a fun, memorable touch that keeps guests fuelled and the celebration flowing.
