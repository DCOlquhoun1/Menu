Embroidery & printing

Crests, logos, names and numbers — digitised, stitched and pressed in our own workshop in Barrhead.

Embroidery

Stitched directly into the fabric, so it lasts as long as the garment does. This is the right choice for school crests, blazers, polos, fleeces, jackets and anything that gets washed constantly.

  • Left chest logos, back logos and sleeve details
  • Names and initials
  • Up to around 12 thread colours in one design
  • Survives industrial washing

Your logo is digitised once into a stitch file. After that, reorders cost nothing extra to set up.

Vinyl printing

Heat-pressed vinyl gives clean, bold lettering at any size. It’s the better option for large back prints, squad numbers, one-off t-shirts and anything with fine detail or big blocks of colour.

  • Large back prints and slogans
  • Names and squad numbers on sportswear
  • Metallic, glitter and neon finishes
  • Cost-effective for small runs and one-offs

Ideal for leavers hoodies, hen and stag tops, birthday t-shirts and charity events.
