Alterations & repairs

Decades of professional sewing experience, on the high street in Barrhead. If it needs shortened, taken in, let out or mended, bring it in.

Everyday alterations

The jobs we do every week

Most alterations are straightforward and quick. Bring the garment in with the shoes or belt you’ll wear it with, we’ll pin it on you, and you’ll usually have it back within the week.

  • Trousers and jeans shortened, with the original hem kept where you want it
  • Skirts and dresses taken up or let down
  • Waistbands taken in or let out
  • Sleeves shortened, including jacket sleeves at the cuff
  • Side seams taken in for a proper fit through the body
  • Zips replaced in trousers, skirts, dresses, coats and boots
  • Linings replaced, pockets repaired, buttons and fastenings
  • Tears, seams and rips mended, patches and knee repairs

Before you come in

  • Bring the shoes you’ll wear with it — hem length depends on them
  • Wear or bring any underwear or belt that changes the fit
  • Wash the garment first if you can
  • Tell us the date you need it for, and we’ll be honest about whether it’s possible

Prices depend on the garment and the work involved, so we quote when we see it. There’s never any obligation — if it isn’t worth altering, we’ll tell you that too.

Occasionwear

Bridal, prom and formalwear

Wedding dresses, bridesmaids, prom dresses, kilts and suits — the outfits where the fit matters most and there is no second chance. We take these in by appointment so you get the time and privacy the job deserves.

  • Bridal gown fitting, taking in, hemming and bustles
  • Bridesmaid and flower girl dresses altered to match
  • Prom dresses taken in, shortened and restyled
  • Suits, jackets and kilt outfits fitted for the day
  • Restyling and remaking of an heirloom dress

Please allow plenty of time. Bridal work is usually two to three fittings, and the summer months book up early. Call us as soon as you have the dress.

Made to measure

Audrey’s Creations started with dressmaking, and we still make garments from scratch when the shops don’t have what you need.

  • Dresses and outfits made to your measurements
  • Dance and show costumes
  • Christening gowns and special occasion pieces
  • Copying a much-loved garment that’s worn out

Made-to-measure work is quoted individually. Bring in a picture of what you have in mind and we’ll talk it through.

Soft furnishings

Curtains, blinds and cushions

The same workshop, scaled up. We shorten, reline and remake curtains, and make new ones to your window measurements in your own fabric or one we source.

  • Curtains shortened, narrowed and relined
  • New curtains made to measure, pencil pleat or eyelet
  • Blackout linings added
  • Roman blinds
  • Cushion covers, throws and table linen
  • Repairs to headings and hooks
Measuring up

How to measure your windows

Measure the pole or track

Not the window — the pole. Measure end to end, excluding finials.

Measure the drop

From the top of the pole (or the eye of the ring) down to where you want the curtain to finish.

Bring the numbers in

With those two measurements and your fabric choice we can quote on the spot.

Bring it in and we’ll take a look

No appointment needed for everyday alterations — just pop into the Barrhead shop during opening hours. Bridal and made-to-measure work is by appointment.
