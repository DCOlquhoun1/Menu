About Audrey’s Creations

A family-run sewing and schoolwear business on Aurs Drive in Barrhead, trading as A.C. Schoolwear.

Our story

It started with a sewing machine

Audrey’s Creations began as an alterations and dressmaking service — hems, zips, wedding dresses and the odd pair of curtains — run on skill, word of mouth and a lot of late nights.

Local parents started asking whether we could put a school badge on a jumper. Then a school asked. Then another. Today A.C. Schoolwear supplies badged uniform to primary and secondary schools across East Renfrewshire, Glasgow, Renfrewshire and further afield, and we’ve added a second store in Castlemilk — but the alterations bench is still at the heart of the shop, and Audrey is still the one at it.

That combination is the whole point of us. Buy the blazer and have the sleeves taken up in the same visit. Buy a size up in August and bring it back in February to be let down. Get the crest changed when your youngest inherits it. Chain retailers can’t do that. We do it every day.

Why shop local

What you get here that you don’t get online

Someone who measures your child

Sizing charts are averages. A ten-minute fitting in store beats three rounds of ordering, returning and reordering in the week before term starts.

A workshop in the same building

Nothing gets posted away. Badging, hemming and repairs all happen here, which is why turnaround is measured in days rather than weeks.

A shop that remembers you

We know which schools want which tie, which blazers run small, and what you bought last year. That’s not a database — that’s just how a local shop works.

Uniform that lasts the year

We’d rather sell you one good blazer that gets altered twice than three cheap ones. It works out cheaper and it looks better.

Honest answers

If a repair isn’t worth doing, or a garment won’t take embroidery, we’ll say so before you spend the money.

Support for local schools

We work with parent councils and school offices on uniform days, leavers orders and staff kit — and we sponsor local teams where we can.
