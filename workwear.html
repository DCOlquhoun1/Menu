Workwear

Branded uniform for small businesses, trades, salons, nurseries and clubs — supplied and badged locally, from a single polo shirt upwards.

For local business

No minimum order, no set-up runaround

Most workwear suppliers want you to order fifty of everything before they’ll talk to you. We don’t. Bring us your logo — or a photo of your van — and we’ll digitise it once, stitch a sample, and then run whatever quantity you actually need, whether that’s three fleeces or three hundred.

Because the embroidery and printing are done here in Barrhead, you can add a new starter’s polo next week without paying set-up costs all over again.

How it works

From logo to finished order

Send your logo

Email us the best version you have. A vector file (AI, EPS, PDF) is ideal, but a good clear image is usually enough to work from.

We quote garments and branding

We’ll suggest garments to suit the job and price them with the embroidery or print included — no hidden set-up surprises.

Approve a sample

For embroidery we digitise your logo and stitch it out first, so you can see the real thing before we run the order.

We run the order

Typical turnaround is one to two weeks depending on garment stock and quantity. Rush jobs — ask, we’ll tell you honestly.

Reorder any time

Your logo stays on file. New starter, or a replacement after a bad day on site? Just call.

Start a workwear enquiry

Get your team looking the part

Tell us what you do and roughly how many people you need to kit out, and we’ll come back with options and prices.
