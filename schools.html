We badge to order for any school

The list above covers the schools we hold stock badges for. That isn’t the limit of what we can do — if you bring us your school’s crest, or the school sends us their artwork, we’ll digitise it once and embroider it onto whatever the uniform list calls for.

  • Nursery and primary polos, cardigans and sweatshirts
  • Secondary blazers, ties, knitwear and PE kit
  • Parent council and PTA orders, including bulk pricing
  • Leavers hoodies with the full class list on the back

For schools and parent councils

We work directly with schools across the west of Scotland to supply and badge uniform. That can mean anything from stocking your crest so parents can walk in and buy, to running a one-off order for a P7 leavers group.

Talk to us about:

  • Becoming your named local uniform supplier
  • Sale days and fitting sessions at the school
  • Staff polos, fleeces and lanyards
  • Sports teams, clubs and after-school groups
