Schoolwear

Everything on the uniform list, badged in our own workshop and fitted properly before you take it home.

Uniform

School-specific and plain uniform under one roof

We stock the badged items your school requires alongside the plain basics that go with them, so one visit covers the whole list. Everything is tried on in store — brand sizing varies far more than parents expect, and a blazer that fits in August is worth more than one that arrives in a bag.

Where a garment needs shortening, taking in or letting out, our alterations workshop is in the same building. Most uniform alterations are back with you within the week.

Find your school

Typical uniform list

  • Badged blazer
  • School tie (standard, clip-on or house colours)
  • Badged or plain knitwear — jumper, cardigan, sweatshirt
  • Shirts and blouses, long and short sleeve
  • Trousers, skirts, pinafores and shorts
  • Polo shirts for primary and nursery
  • Socks, tights and accessories
  • Book bags, gym bags and rucksacks

Stock varies by school and time of year. Call ahead if you need something specific in a hurry.

PE & sportswear

Kit that survives the school year

PE kit takes more punishment than anything else in the bag. We stock hard-wearing tops, shorts and skorts, and we can add names and squad numbers so nothing goes missing in the changing room.

PE tops & shorts

Badged and plain PE tops, shorts, skorts and base layers in school colours, in child and adult sizing.

Team & club kit

Football, netball, hockey and athletics kit for school teams and local clubs — embroidered crests and printed numbers.

Names on everything

Printed or embroidered names on tops, shorts, bags and towels. The single best thing you can do about lost property.

Leavers hoodies

Class of … hoodies, done properly

P7 and S6 leavers hoodies are one of our favourite jobs of the year. Embroidered school crest and name on the front, the full class list or year group printed on the back, and a nickname on the sleeve if the school allows it.

  • Any size from child small to adult 3XL in one order
  • Individual names embroidered or printed
  • Bulk pricing for whole year groups
  • Parent council and school-run orders welcome

Dancewear

Dance and gymnastics

Alongside schoolwear we supply dancewear for local classes and shows — leotards, tights, skirts, shoes and accessories — and we make and alter costumes to fit.

  • Leotards, crossovers, skirts and tutus
  • Ballet, tap and jazz shoes
  • Tights, socks and hair accessories
  • Costume making, taking in and repairs before a show

Running a dance school? We can badge kit with your studio logo and hold stock so parents can buy direct.
